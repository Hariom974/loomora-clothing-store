Our story

Built by people who wear their own gear

Loomora started as a two-person workshop making replacement workwear for people who couldn't find clothes that survived a real day's work. Every piece is still tested in-house before it goes on the rack.

No seasonal gimmicks, no throwaway fabric — just garments designed to be worn hard and repaired, not replaced.
